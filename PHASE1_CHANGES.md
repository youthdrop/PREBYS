# Youth Drop-In MIS — Phase 1

Phase 1 includes:

- Youth-centered dashboard with active youth, contacts due, activities, and recent case notes.
- Youth intake with demographics, birthdate-based age, emergency contact, status, next contact, and staff assignment.
- Searchable youth directory with assigned staff and next-contact information.
- Editable youth profile.
- Staff reassignment for administrators, supervisors, and managers; regular staff remain limited to assigned cases at the API level.
- Rolling case note timeline with contact method, note type, next action, next contact date, author, timestamp, and confidential flag.
- Employment and school verification upload/download retained on the youth profile.

After replacing your local project, restart the backend and frontend. Log out and back in to refresh the administrator display name.
